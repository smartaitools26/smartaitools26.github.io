Privacy Policy

Smart AI Tools respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles data, ads, AI features, notes, permissions, notifications, QR scanning, and third-party services.

1. Personal Data

We do not collect or store personal information such as name, email, phone number, address, or payment information directly within the app.

2. AI Services

The app uses internet access to process user input through AI services in order to generate, summarize, or assist with text-based content.

User input may be sent to third-party AI providers for processing.

Please avoid entering sensitive, private, confidential, financial, medical, legal, or personal information into the app.

AI-generated content may not always be accurate or reliable. Users are responsible for reviewing and verifying results before use.

3. Notes

Notes created inside the app are stored locally on the user’s device only. We do not upload, access, read, or store your notes on our servers.

If the app is deleted or app data is cleared, notes may be permanently lost.

4. QR Scanner

The app may use camera access to scan QR codes only when the user chooses to use this feature.

We do not store or upload camera data or scanned images.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads.

AdMob may collect and use data such as advertising ID, device information, approximate location, app usage data, and ad interaction data to improve ad relevance and performance.

Users can opt out of personalized ads: Google Ads Settings

More details: Google Privacy Policy

6. Firebase Services

We use Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and notifications.

Firebase may collect technical data such as device information, crash logs, usage statistics, and performance data.

7. Notifications

The app may send notifications for updates, reminders, and important messages.

Users can disable notifications anytime from device settings.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share users’ personal data with third parties.

Some third-party services used by the app may process limited technical data according to their own privacy policies.

9. Internet Access

Internet access is required for AI features, ads, analytics, notifications, crash reporting, QR link handling, and app functionality.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Users are responsible for the information they provide.

11. Children

The app is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

12. Third-Party Services

This app may use third-party services including Google AdMob, Firebase, and AI providers.

These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

13. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • - Internet: required for AI features, ads, analytics, and services
  • - Notifications: used for updates, reminders, and alerts
  • - Camera: used only for QR scanning when the user chooses this feature

14. User Consent

By using Smart AI Tools, you agree to this Privacy Policy and understand that third-party services may process limited technical data as described above.

15. Policy Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Smart AI Tools application.

16. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
