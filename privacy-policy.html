Privacy Policy

Smart AI Tools respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles data, ads, AI features, notes, QR scanner features, permissions, and third-party services.

1. Personal Data

We do not collect or store personal information such as name, email, phone number, address, or payment information directly within the app.

2. AI Services

The app uses internet access to process user input through AI services in order to generate, summarize, or assist with text-based content.

User input may be sent to third-party AI providers for processing. Please avoid entering sensitive, private, confidential, financial, medical, legal, or personal information into the app.

AI-generated content may not always be accurate, complete, or suitable for every purpose. Users are responsible for reviewing and verifying generated content before using it.

3. Notes

Notes created inside the app are stored locally on the user’s device only. We do not upload, access, read, or store your notes on our servers.

If the app is deleted or app data is cleared, locally saved notes may be permanently removed from the device.

4. QR Scanner

The app may include a QR scanner feature. Camera access is used only to scan QR codes when the user chooses to use this feature.

We do not record, store, or upload camera images or videos through the QR scanner feature.

5. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect non-personal data such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and app usage data to improve ad quality and relevance.

Users can opt out of personalized ads by visiting: https://adssettings.google.com

For more information, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Firebase Services

The app may use Firebase services such as Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, and Cloud Messaging to improve performance, detect issues, understand app usage, and send notifications.

Firebase may collect limited technical data such as device type, operating system version, crash reports, performance data, and app interaction information.

7. Notifications

The app may request notification permission in order to send important updates, reminders, or app-related messages.

Users can disable notifications at any time from their device settings.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share personal data. Some third-party services may process limited technical data according to their own privacy policies.

9. Internet Access

Internet access is required for AI features, ads, notifications, analytics, app performance improvements, and third-party services.

10. Security

We aim to provide a secure experience, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Users are responsible for the information they enter into the app.

11. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Services

This app may use third-party services including Google AdMob, Firebase, and AI service providers. These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

13. Permissions

The app may request permissions such as internet access, camera access, and notifications to function properly.

Camera permission is used for QR scanning only. Notification permission is used to send app-related messages. Internet permission is used for AI, ads, Firebase services, and online features.

14. User Consent

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to the use of the services and permissions described above.

15. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
